Our Heritage

A Legacy of
Timeless Elegance

Verdana Grand Hotel & Spa was born from a vision to create a sanctuary where the raw magnificence of the Himalayas meets the refined artistry of luxury hospitality. Established in 1998 by the Verdana family, our hotel has stood as a beacon of elegance in the colonial hills of Shimla for over two and a half decades.

Every corridor tells a story. From hand-carved Himachali woodwork adorning our ceilings to the locally sourced organic produce gracing our fine-dining tables, every detail has been curated with intention. We believe luxury is not merely an experience — it is a philosophy.

Our 120 rooms and suites are designed as private retreats, offering panoramic views of Jakhu Hill and the snow-dusted Himalayan ranges. Whether you seek the serenity of our award-winning spa, the thrill of mountain excursions, or the simple pleasure of afternoon tea on your private balcony — Verdana Grand is your home among the clouds.
